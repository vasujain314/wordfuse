Welcome to Word Fuse, a fast word game with a typewriter look!

• Get a few letters, type a real word that contains them before the fuse burns out
• 2 lives, 10 seconds a word, and it gets harder as you score
• Play with friends: host a room, share the link, up to 8 players take turns live
• Friends can join from the app or any web browser
• Missed one? See a word that would have worked
• 168,000-word dictionary, no sign-up needed
